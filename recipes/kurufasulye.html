Kurufasulye

Description

Kurufasulye is a classic stew of white beans in a rich tomato sauce, slowly cooked with onion, garlic and bell pepper. Popular in both Turkish and East African cuisine, served with rice or bread.

Ingredients

  • 400 g dried white beans (or 2 cans, drained)
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 400 g peeled tomatoes (canned) or 4 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper and paprika powder to taste

Steps

  1. Soak the dried beans overnight in water, then cook for 1-1.5 hours until tender. (Skip if using canned beans.)
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion for 5 minutes until soft and translucent.
  3. Add garlic and bell pepper, cook for 3 more minutes. Stir in the tomato paste.
  4. Add the tomatoes, paprika powder, salt and pepper. Let simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the beans and let everything gently simmer together for 15-20 minutes. Serve with rice or bread.
