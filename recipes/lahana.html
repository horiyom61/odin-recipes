Lahana Corbasi

Description

Lahana Çorbasi is a hearty Turkish cabbage soup, slow-cooked with vegetables and aromatic spices. It is a beloved comfort food in Turkish cuisine, especially popular during cold winters. The soup is rich, warming, and full of flavor.

Ingredients

  • ½ medium white cabbage, roughly chopped
  • 2 medium carrots, sliced
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1.5 liters vegetable or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper and red pepper flakes to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté the onion and garlic for 5 minutes until soft and translucent.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 more minutes.
  3. Add the carrots and cabbage to the pot and stir well to coat everything with the tomato mixture.
  4. Pour in the broth and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and let simmer for 25-30 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and red pepper flakes to taste. Serve hot with crusty bread.
