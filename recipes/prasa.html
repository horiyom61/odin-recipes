Prasa

Description

Prasa is a classic Turkish and Greek dish of leeks slowly braised in olive oil with carrots and rice. It is served at room temperature as a meze or side dish. Simple, earthy and deeply satisfying, it is a staple of home cooking across the Eastern Mediterranean.

Ingredients

  • 4 large leeks, cleaned and sliced into thick rounds
  • 2 medium carrots, sliced
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp long-grain rice
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Sauté the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the carrots and leeks and stir gently to coat them in the oil.
  3. Stir in the rice, sugar, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Pour in enough water to just cover the vegetables (about 200 ml).
  4. Cover the pan with a lid, reduce the heat to low and cook for 30–35 minutes until the leeks are very tender and the liquid is mostly absorbed.
  5. Remove from heat and let cool to room temperature before serving. Drizzle with extra olive oil and a squeeze of lemon if desired.
